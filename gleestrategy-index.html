Services

Fractional Leadership

Organizations in transition need more than a placeholder. They need someone who can assess quickly and lead effectively. Drawing on decades of nonprofit leadership at the executive level, I step into a position to evaluate its needs, review the board's program standards, and listen closely to staff before crafting solutions. From there, I help train and mentor the incoming leader, so the transition strengthens the organization rather than stalling it.

Board Training

Many boards struggle to find their collective voice, or to know how to interpret and act on the information presented at meetings. I offer facilitated sessions designed to help board members better understand their roles, ask questions they may hesitate to raise in a formal meeting, and recognize the distinct value each of them brings to the table.

Federal & Sponsored Research Program Assistance

Securing federal grants from DoD, NIH, HHS, and other agencies is only the first step. Tracking, reporting, and maintaining required standard operating procedures before and after funds are accepted is where many organizations run into trouble. I help resolve existing compliance issues and mentor leadership through the process, so funding becomes an asset, not a liability.
